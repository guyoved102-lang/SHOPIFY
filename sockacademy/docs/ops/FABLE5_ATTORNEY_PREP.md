## Specific open questions

**Q1 — Governing law / forum.** The ToS specifies Delaware law and AAA consumer arbitration, but the operator is an **Israeli individual with no US entity**. Is Delaware governing law + AAA arbitration appropriate and practically enforceable for this structure, or should governing law be Israel (or something else) until a US entity exists? If Delaware is kept, does anything need to change before vs. after entity formation?

**Q2 — Arbitration clause enforceability.** Are the class-action waiver and the one-year claims limitation (ToS §14) enforceable against US consumers as drafted, and do they create problems under Israeli consumer-protection law for any Israeli customers?

**Q3 — GDPR adequacy.** Is Privacy Policy §5–§6.1 (SCC reliance, 30-day response, listed rights) sufficient for incidental EU/UK traffic to a US-facing store, or is more required (e.g., EU representative under GDPR Art. 27, cookie consent banner beyond browser-settings language, lawful-basis mapping)? Note: the policy says users "consent by using the Site" (§1) — is that consent framing acceptable under GDPR?

**Q4 — CCPA/FTC.** (a) Does the store, pre-revenue, even meet CCPA/CPRA thresholds — and if not, is it safer to keep or drop §6.2? (b) FTC angle: dropshipping is disclosed as "international logistics providers" with 7–25-day delivery windows — does this satisfy FTC Mail Order Rule expectations, and is the "at our discretion" lost-package remedy acceptable?

**Q5 — Medical/therapeutic claims boundary (live issue).** A quarantine already exists: `docs/strategy/research_materials.md` lines 177–186 fence off bamboo "antibacterial"/Bamboo Kun claims and the entire copper-infused-fiber section (MRSA, athlete's foot, "medical-grade athletic and diabetic socks") behind a REGULATORY HOLD banner so they never reach customer copy. **However, three adjacent items still exist outside the quarantine** and need a ruling on where the line is:
   - `agents/A3_content/agent.js` line 56 — blog topic "Copper-Infused Socks: The Science Behind the Hype" with keyword "antimicrobial socks" sits in the live topic rotation.
   - `agents/A5_social/agent.js` line 118 — Instagram topic "Antimicrobial fibers — copper-infused socks decoded."
   - `scripts/setup/content-generator.js` (~lines 444–460) — a full pre-written "diabetic socks" health article (neuropathy statistics, infection-risk claims) in a manual, publish-off-by-default setup script.
   Question: which claim types (odor resistance, "inhibits bacterial growth," antimicrobial, anything referencing diabetes/medical conditions) are permissible in editorial/blog content for a general-wellness sock brand, and which trigger FTC/FDA substantiation requirements? (Practical intent: delete or rewrite the three items above per your answer.)

**Q6 — MG-2 clearance.** Reviewing the Arch Zone excerpt above and the full section (MG-2-FINAL.md lines 49–59): is the "structure not therapy" framing sufficient to avoid the regulated compression-garment category, or does it need edits? A written yes/no lifts or keeps the publish hold.

**Q7 — Entity & tax timing.** Given a US-facing store run by an Israeli sole proprietor: (a) is it acceptable to launch and take first revenue before forming an entity, and at what point does that flip; (b) any immediate Israeli consumer-protection or VAT registration obligations triggered by first USD sales; (c) what should the ENTITY placeholder legally read until formation (e.g., "SockAcademy, a sole proprietorship of [name]")?

**Q8 — Trademark clearance — CONFIRMED REGISTERED MARK FOUND, plus one adjacent mark (updated 06/07/2026, Guy's own self-search across UK IPO + TMview/WIPO Global Brand DB).** A UK company "Sock Academy Ltd" (Companies House 05743003, active, incorporated 15/03/2006, formerly "United Oddsocks Limited") operates `sockacademy.com` and sells novelty socks in a claimed 18 countries. **We have directly confirmed, cross-checked via two independent sources (UK IPO's own register and TMview/WIPO Global Brand DB), that they hold a REGISTERED UK trademark: `UK00003187452`, mark text "Sock Academy," applicant "Sock Academy Limited," status Registered, filed 23/09/2016, registered 23/12/2016, expiring 23/09/2026, Class 25, goods "Socks and clothing."** This is the primary, directly-conflicting mark.

A TMview "contains SOCK ACADEMY" search across all participating offices (which returned 5 total hits) also surfaced: (a) a **separate, unrelated company "Dance Sock Academy Limited"** holding registered Class 25 marks for "DANCE SOCK ACADEMY LTD" in **New Zealand** (IPONZ #1143757, reg. 19/03/2020, goods: socks, dance clothing, sports socks, ballet shoes/footwear) and **Australia** (IPA #2199084, reg. 31/07/2021, same goods) — a different mark name, lower relevance, but worth noting since it shows this style of name has registered precedent in Class 25 in two more English-speaking jurisdictions; and (b) two clearly irrelevant substring-match false positives (a UK dance-tuition mark "...sock & pop academy" in Class 41, and an Italian optical company's "Socket Academy" logo mark in Class 41) — neither is a real conflict for a sock/clothing e-commerce business and both can be disregarded.

A parallel US search (USPTO wordmark search for "SOCKACADEMY") returned zero live or dead records — no apparent US conflict found by us, though this was a self-service search, not a professional clearance. The TMview cross-office search likewise surfaced no EUIPO (EU-wide) or USPTO hits for "Sock Academy," suggesting (not confirming) no registered EU-wide or US mark exists. Israel (ILPO) self-search was inconclusive (input/filter error) and was not successfully re-run.

Please run or commission a full professional knock-out clearance search for SOCK ACADEMY / SOCKACADEMY in Class 25 for the US, UK, EU, Australia, New Zealand, and Israel — treating the confirmed UK registration (`UK00003187452`) as the baseline fact, not a hypothesis — and advise: (a) risk of continued use of the name given the confirmed UK registration, especially if we ever sell into or ship to the UK/EU; (b) whether the separate "Dance Sock Academy" AU/NZ registrations add any meaningful additional risk given the name difference; (c) whether our own trademark registration (already planned for Phase 3A per the roadmap) is likely to succeed or likely to be blocked/opposed, particularly in the UK/EU; (d) how urgently a rebrand conversation should happen — cost of checking now is near-zero; cost of a forced rebrand after real traction would be the most expensive event in this project's history.
