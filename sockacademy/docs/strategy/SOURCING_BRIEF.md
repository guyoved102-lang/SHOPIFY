## 1. About SockAcademy

SockAcademy is a direct-to-consumer premium sock brand. We are building a private label product line focused on technical construction, certified fiber sourcing, and long-term customer relationships through a subscription replenishment model.

We are not a fashion brand. We are not a promotional product buyer. We source once, we source correctly, and we maintain long-term supplier relationships with the manufacturers who meet our standards.

Our customer is an adult professional who purchases premium footwear and expects the same standard in hosiery. Price point: $30–45 USD per pair at retail.

We are currently evaluating 4–6 manufacturers. Selection is based on technical capability and quality consistency, not price alone.

## 2. Academy Grade Standard — Technical Specification

The following specifications are **non-negotiable minimums**. Submissions that cannot confirm all items will not proceed to sample stage.

### 2.1 Fiber Composition (Per Colorway)

| Component | Specification |
|---|---|
| Primary fiber | Merino wool, ≤18.5 micron |
| Wool certification | ZQ Merino certified, or documentation of equivalent certified fiber source |
| Nylon reinforcement | 15–20% (heel and toe zones) |
| Elastane/Spandex | 2–5% (for elasticity recovery) |
| Cotton content | Zero. Not permitted in any zone. |
| Synthetic blend | Not permitted as primary fiber substitute. |

*Note: Please confirm the wool source country and certification body. We will request certificates during the evaluation phase.*

### 2.2 Construction Requirements

| Specification | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Knitting machine | 200 needle count minimum |
| Toe seam | Linked (seamless, hand-linked or machine-linked). Looped seam is not acceptable. |
| Heel construction | Structured heel pocket (reinforced cup). Flat heel not acceptable. |
| Arch zone | Graduated compression band (not medical compression — structural only) |
| Leg ribbing | 2×1 or 3×1 rib minimum |
| Cuff tension | Consistent across width — no roll or collapse after 5 wash cycles |

### 2.3 Yarn and Finishing

| Specification | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Yarn twist | Consistent twist per ply across colorways |
| Superwash treatment | Merino must be Superwash-treated (machine wash safe) |
| Dimensional stability | ≤5% shrinkage after 5 washes at 30°C |
| Colorfastness | Grade 4 minimum (ISO 105-C06) |
| Pilling resistance | ≥8,000 Martindale cycles |
| Weight per pair | Within ±3g of spec weight per colorway |

### 2.4 Private Label Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Brand label | Woven (not printed, not heat-transfer) |
| Label position | Inside back of cuff — consistent placement |
| Label content | Brand name + fiber content + micron count + country of origin |
| Hang tag | Plain card, white — we supply print-ready artwork |
| Custom colorways | Ability to match Pantone references provided by us |
| MOQ (initial) | Minimum order: 200 pairs per colorway (negotiable at volume) |

## 3. Quality Control Requirements

The following checklist will be applied to every inbound shipment by our QC team. Please confirm your internal QC process against each item before submitting.

### 3.1 Pre-Shipment QC Checklist

**Fiber & Construction**
- [ ] Fiber composition matches contracted spec (we will conduct random fiber testing)
- [ ] Micron count verified against ZQ or equivalent certification document
- [ ] Knitting consistency: no dropped stitches, no snags across full batch
- [ ] Linked toe seam: smooth inner profile, no raised seam line
- [ ] Heel pocket depth consistent across full batch (measure 5% sample)

**Dimensional**
- [ ] Pair weight within ±3g of spec
- [ ] Length within ±3mm of size spec
- [ ] Cuff width consistent (no flaring or narrowing)
- [ ] Dimensional stability confirmed post-wash cycle (wash test included in sample order)

**Finishing**
- [ ] Woven label: no loose threads, no misalignment
- [ ] Label content complete and legible: brand, fiber, country
- [ ] Colorfastness: no visible bleed after 30°C wash
- [ ] Pilling: no surface pilling visible after 5 wash cycles
- [ ] Pair matching: both socks in pair identical in weight and shade

**Packaging** *(we supply specs — you execute)*
- [ ] Hangtag attached correctly (position per artwork)
- [ ] Pairs folded per our packaging SOP (to be provided)
- [ ] Polybag: recyclable, no branded printing on polybag

### 3.2 Batch Rejection Criteria
Any shipment with more than 2% defect rate on the above checklist will be returned or credited. We expect your internal QC to produce below 0.5% defect rate.

## 5. Sample Request Protocol

If you wish to be considered for this partnership, please supply the following:

### 5.1 Sample Requirements
- **Quantity:** 3 pairs per colorway
- **Colorway:** Charcoal (Pantone 19-0303 TCX) or closest production match
- **Size:** EU 42–44 / US 9–11
- **Construction:** Per Section 2 specifications
- **Lead time:** Please confirm sample production lead time

### 5.2 Required Documentation with Sample
- [ ] Fiber composition certificate (ZQ or equivalent)
- [ ] Needle count of knitting machine used
- [ ] Current ISO 9001 certificate (if held)
- [ ] Minimum order quantity per colorway
- [ ] Production lead time from confirmed PO
- [ ] Payment terms

### 5.3 Sample Evaluation Process
We will evaluate samples across 5 criteria:
1. Hand feel and touch (initial impression)
2. Seam quality (linked toe under magnification)
3. Dimensional stability (5 wash cycles at 30°C)
4. Color consistency vs. Pantone reference
5. Construction integrity post-wash (pilling, shape retention)

We will provide written evaluation feedback within 14 business days of receipt.
