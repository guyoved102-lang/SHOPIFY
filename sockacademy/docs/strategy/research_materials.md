## MERINO WOOL

### Grade Classification (by fiber diameter)
| Grade | Micron (μ) | Classification | Use Case |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ultra-fine | ≤ 15.5μ | Cashmere-equivalent | Luxury next-to-skin |
| Super-fine | 15.6–18.5μ | Premium next-to-skin | Dress & formal socks |
| Fine | 18.6–20.0μ | High performance | Athletic, everyday premium |
| Medium | 20.1–23.0μ | Standard performance | Outdoor, hiking |
| Broad | > 23.0μ | Coarse | Work, heavy-duty |

**The 18.5μ threshold** — the scientific boundary below which wool fiber ceases to cause skin irritation. The Medullated Fiber Percentage drops to near zero. This is the grade SockAcademy targets.

### Origin Profiles
- **New Zealand (Merino):** Merino sheep graze at altitude on South Island highlands. Lower temperatures slow fiber growth, producing finer, longer staple. ZQ Merino certified. Traceable to individual station.
- **Australia (Merino):** Predominantly Peppin Merino breed. Strong AWTA testing infrastructure. Woolmark certified. Best volume at 18.5–19.5μ.
- **South Africa (Cape Merino):** High UV exposure produces unique fiber brightness. Rarer at ultra-fine grades.
- **Patagonia (Argentina):** Rugged terrain, similar fiber profile to NZ. Growing premium segment.

### Construction Properties
- **Moisture-wicking:** Merino absorbs up to 35% of its weight in moisture before feeling wet. Wicks away from skin via capillary action.
- **Temperature regulation:** Hollow fiber core traps air when cold; opens structure releases heat when warm. Active thermoregulation across 5–35°C.
- **Odor resistance:** Lanolin content + protein structure inhibits bacterial growth. Wear multiple days without odor.
- **Biodegradable:** 100% natural. Decomposes in soil within 1 year.
- **Shrink resistance:** Superwash treatment (chlorine-Hercosett or ozone process) prevents felt shrinkage. Machine-washable.

### Certifications to Reference
- **ZQ Merino** — Gold standard. Mulesing-free, animal welfare, land sustainability, fiber quality.
- **Woolmark** — Quality assurance, fiber purity.
- **GOTS (Global Organic Textile Standard)** — Organic processing chain.
- **Responsible Wool Standard (RWS)** — Land and animal welfare.

### Editorial Copy (ready to use)
> "At 18.5 microns, the fiber is narrower than the human hair's medullary threshold — the point at which wool transitions from abrasive to imperceptible. Below this line, the skin registers only softness."

> "Merino does not merely insulate. It actively manages the microclimate between sock and skin — absorbing perspiration before it becomes moisture, releasing it as vapor. A thermodynamic system, engineered by altitude and cold."

## EGYPTIAN COTTON

### Variety Classification
| Variety | Staple Length | Grade | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Giza 45 | 36–38mm | Extra-long staple (ELS) | The benchmark. Produced near Nile Delta. |
| Giza 87 | 35–37mm | ELS | Most widely cultivated premium grade |
| Giza 88 | 34–36mm | Long staple | High volume, still premium |
| Giza 70 | 38–40mm | Ultra ELS | Rarest. Finest sheen. Near-extinct crop. |

**ELS threshold:** 28mm+. Below this, cotton is "standard." Luxury socks require 34mm minimum.

### Why the Nile Delta
Alluvial soil deposits from annual Nile flooding create mineral-rich earth unmatched globally. Combined with 3,000+ hours of annual sun and controlled irrigation, cotton grown here develops longer, finer fibers with a natural silk-like luster.

**Key distinction from imitations:** "Egyptian Cotton" is not a protected designation of origin in most markets. Genuine Giza varieties require CCPO (Cotton Egypt Association) certification. Without it, the claim is marketing.

### Properties
- **Luster:** Natural silk-like sheen from longer fiber alignment in yarn.
- **Breathability:** Lower fiber count per cross-section allows greater airflow than short-staple cotton.
- **Pilling resistance:** Longer fibers mean fewer fiber ends exposed on surface. Minimal pilling over time.
- **Strength:** ELS fibers have higher tensile strength — socks hold shape wash after wash.
- **Absorbency:** 27× its weight in water. Superior moisture management for all-day wear.
- **Softness progression:** Unlike synthetic blends, ELS cotton becomes softer with each wash as fibers align.

### Certifications to Reference
- **CCPO (Cotton Egypt Association)** — Only legitimate certification for authentic Giza cotton.
- **Supima** — American ELS equivalent (Pima cotton from US Southwest). Not Egyptian, but equivalent quality.
- **OEKO-TEX Standard 100** — No harmful substances in final product.

### Editorial Copy (ready to use)
> "Egyptian cotton does not announce itself. It arrives quietly — in the way a sock settles without adjustment, in the absence of friction, in the feel of a fabric that has simply been made correctly."

> "The Nile Delta has been cultivating this cotton for four millennia. The soil remembers what synthetics attempt to simulate. Extra-long staple fibers, grown slowly under 3,000 hours of annual sun, align into a yarn of such uniformity that the resulting textile approaches silk in hand-feel."

## PIMA COTTON (SUPIMA)

### Classification
- **Pima:** Extra-long staple cotton (ELS) grown in US Southwest (Arizona, California, New Mexico, Texas), Peru, and Australia.
- **Supima:** Trademarked designation for American-grown Pima. Licensed to brands. Protected name.
- **Peruvian Pima:** Grown in coastal valleys. Hand-picked. Comparable quality to Supima with distinct softness profile.

### Properties
- Staple length: 34–38mm (ELS)
- Fiber strength: 25–30% stronger than standard upland cotton
- Luster: High — comparable to Egyptian Giza 88
- Shrinkage: Minimal when pre-washed
- Color retention: Superior dye uptake due to longer fiber surface area

### vs. Egyptian Cotton
| Property | Pima/Supima | Egyptian Giza |
|---|---|---|
| Staple length | 34–38mm | 34–40mm |
| Luster | High | Very high |
| Availability | Good (US/Peru) | Limited (Egypt) |
| Price point | Premium | Ultra-premium |
| Certification | Supima® trademark | CCPO |

### Editorial Copy (ready to use)
> "Supima cotton represents fewer than 1% of all cotton grown in the United States. It is not a marketing category — it is a licensed designation, traceable to specific farms in the American Southwest. Every Supima sock begins with a fiber that most mills never touch."

## CASHMERE BLENDS

### Grade System
| Grade | Fiber Diameter | Region | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grade A | 14–15.5μ | Inner Mongolia | Finest. Limited production. |
| Grade B | 15.5–18μ | China/Mongolia | Standard luxury grade |
| Grade C | 18–20μ | Iran/Afghanistan | Commercial grade |

### Sock Applications
Pure cashmere in socks is impractical — low durability, high pilling. Premium cashmere socks use:
- **Cashmere/Nylon blend:** 85/15 — luxury hand-feel with structural durability
- **Cashmere/Merino blend:** 70/30 — warmth amplification, natural fiber softness
- **Cashmere/Silk blend:** 80/20 — dress sock benchmark

## CONSTRUCTION SPECIFICATIONS

### Gauge (Needles per Inch)
| Gauge | Needles | Result | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low (100–120 needle) | Coarse | Thick, cushioned | Hiking, tactical |
| Medium (160–180 needle) | Standard | Balanced | Athletic, everyday |
| High (200 needle) | Fine | Smooth, refined | Business, dress |
| Ultra (240 needle) | Very fine | Near-sheer | Formal, luxury |

**SockAcademy standard:** 200-needle minimum for all dress/premium categories.

### Weave Types
| Weave | Description | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Plain rib | 1×1 or 2×2 vertical ribs | Classic dress sock — holds shape, elegant |
| Terry loop | Looped pile on interior | Cushioned athletic — moisture management |
| Pointelle | Open-work pattern | Lightweight dress, breathability |
| Jacquard | Complex pattern woven in structure | Premium patterned socks |
| Cable knit | 3D textured pattern | Winter, luxury casual |
| Mesh | Open lattice weave | Hot-weather, ventilation |

### Toe Construction
| Type | Description | Quality Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Single-link | Flat seam, hand-linked | Luxury standard — no toe bulge |
| Loopback | Machine-looped toe | Standard commercial |
| Hand-turned | Individual hand-finishing | Ultra-premium, near-custom |

**SockAcademy standard:** Single-link toe on all premium lines.

### Heel Reinforcement
- **Nylon-reinforced heel:** Most common. Extends lifespan 3–5×.
- **Spandex/Elastane:** Maintains shape and grip (2–5% typical).
- **Cushioned heel tab:** For athletic use — prevents blistering.

## TECHNICAL BLENDS — PERFORMANCE MATERIALS

### Bamboo (Lyocell/Viscose from Bamboo)
- **Moisture-wicking:** Micro-gaps in fiber structure wick 3× faster than cotton.
- **Softness:** Comparable to cashmere at Grade B.
- **Sustainability:** Bamboo regenerates in 3–5 years. No pesticides required.
- **Note:** "Bamboo" must specify processing method — viscose vs. lyocell (closed-loop). Lyocell is superior environmental profile.

## CARE PROTOCOLS (by material)

### Merino Wool
```
Machine wash: Cold (30°C max), gentle cycle
Detergent: Wool-specific (Woolite, Eucalan) — no enzyme-based
Drying: Lay flat — never tumble dry or hang
Ironing: Not required. Steam only if needed at low heat.
Frequency: Every 2–3 wears (odor resistance extends wear)
```

### Egyptian / Pima Cotton
```
Machine wash: 30–40°C, normal cycle
Detergent: Standard mild — avoid bleach
Drying: Tumble dry low, or air dry flat
Ironing: Medium heat, inside out to preserve luster
Frequency: After every wear
```

### Cashmere Blend
```
Machine wash: Cold (20°C), hand-wash cycle OR hand-wash
Detergent: Baby shampoo or cashmere-specific formula
Drying: Roll in towel to remove water — lay flat on mesh
Ironing: Never. Steam at 5cm distance if needed.
Storage: Folded, not hung — cedar block to deter moths
```
