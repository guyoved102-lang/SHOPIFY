# SockAcademy — Private Label Roadmap
**Transition: Dropshipping Vision → Private Label Authority**
**Locked: 01/07/2026. Authority: CTO + CEO.**

---

## Executive Summary

SockAcademy is transitioning from a Shopify store with dropshipping intent to a private label brand built on three pillars: Foot Health authority, Material Science precision, and Editorial credibility. This roadmap defines the exact sequence, capital gates, and quality standards required to execute this transition without compromising the brand foundation already built.

No inventory is purchased before the capital gates in Phase 2 are met. No paid acquisition before Phase 4 content gates are met.

**⚠️ AMENDMENT (01/07/2026):** Guy has clarified that a Bridge Sourcing phase — dropshipping from a vetted, quality supplier (explicitly NOT AliExpress-tier, NOT generic CJ marketplace trend-chasing) — is needed before full private label capital deployment. This qualifies, not reverses, the "no dropshipping" framing above: the objection was never to dropshipping as a mechanism, it was to low-quality, unvetted, high-return-rate suppliers undermining the authority brand before it exists. `A1_product_research` remains LIVE (not gated) to support this. **Open question, not yet resolved:** A1's current logic scans generic CJ Dropshipping trending products across many SKUs — this needs to be re-scoped to source specifically for premium hosiery / Merino-adjacent quality suppliers rather than broad trend-chasing, or it continues producing recommendations misaligned with the one-SKU authority strategy even while technically "allowed" to run. Flagged for Guy's decision on A1's actual sourcing criteria — not yet actioned.

## Phase 1: Supplier Discovery + Sampling
**Timeline:** Month 3 → Month 6

### Objective
Identify 2–3 manufacturers capable of meeting Academy Grade standards. Receive and evaluate physical samples.

### Deliverables
- [ ] Sourcing Brief sent to minimum 5 manufacturers (Turkey, Portugal, Taiwan)
- [ ] Evaluate responses: filter to top 3 by quality signal
- [ ] Sample order placed: 1 colorway per shortlisted manufacturer
- [ ] Sample evaluation protocol completed (see Quality Checklist in SOURCING_BRIEF.md)
- [ ] Wash test × 5 cycles: dimensional stability measured
- [ ] Third-party fiber testing if manufacturer cannot provide lab report
- [ ] Visit or video audit of shortlisted manufacturer's facility
- [ ] Selected manufacturer: signed NDA + exclusivity negotiation begin

### Capital Required
$500–2,000 (samples, shipping, optional lab testing)

### Key Events
- **Texworld Paris (September)** or **Première Vision** — attend if possible
- Alternative: LinkedIn/Alibaba filtered to "private label, ISO 9001, 200+ needle" — then email Sourcing Brief directly

### Gold Discipline
- Packaging direction begins: define mailer box spec before PO discussions
- Never describe brand to manufacturers as "luxury" — use "premium technical" instead

## Phase 2: MOQ Negotiation + Initial PO
**Timeline:** Month 6 → Month 9

### ⚠️ CAPITAL COMMITMENT GATE
**$15,000–28,000 total commitment. Do not proceed until ALL 5 gates below are met.**

### Capital Gates — All Must Be True
| # | Gate | Minimum Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Email subscriber list | ≥ 500 subscribers |
| 2 | Content published | ≥ 6 pieces live (articles or guides) |
| 3 | Manufacturer selected | ≥ 1 passed sample review |
| 4 | Legal structure complete | Business entity + trademark filed |
| 5 | Klaviyo subscription flow operational | Tested end-to-end |

**If any gate is not met → delay PO. Capital risk without demand proof = brand destruction.**

### Deliverables
- [ ] MOQ negotiated: target 200–400 pairs per colorway (Taiwan), 300–500 (Portugal)
- [ ] Launch colorways: 2 maximum (Charcoal + Ivory or Charcoal + Navy)
- [ ] Custom woven label: Academy mark, fiber content, country of origin
- [ ] Packaging spec locked: mailer box, tissue, editorial card, inner tag
- [ ] Initial PO placed: signed contract, 30% deposit
- [ ] Production timeline confirmed: typical 8–14 weeks

### Capital Breakdown (Conservative)
| Line Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Initial inventory (400 pairs × $12/pair avg) | $4,800 |
| Packaging (mailer, tissue, card, tag) × 400 | $1,200–2,400 |
| Customs, shipping, import duties | $800–2,000 |
| Sample revision round | $500–1,000 |
| 3PL setup fee | $500–1,500 |
| Contingency (15%) | $1,200–1,500 |
| **Total** | **$9,000–13,400** |

Add branding/trademark costs ($2,000–5,000) → upper bound reaches $18,000.

### Gold Discipline — Packaging
- **Mailer box:** Matte black, soft-touch laminate, embossed logo (no foil)
- **Tissue:** Charcoal or ivory — no printing on tissue itself
- **Editorial card:** Cream uncoated stock, black text, Academy mark in one corner only
- **Inner tag:** White, Helvetica or similar, fiber content + micron count + country of origin
- **Gold appears once:** Academy Grade mark on editorial card only. Nowhere else.

## Phase 3: 3PL + Quality Control Infrastructure
**Timeline:** Month 9 → Month 11

### Objective
Receive inventory, pass inbound QC, establish fulfillment infrastructure, and prepare subscription flow.

### Deliverables
- [ ] 3PL partner selected: specializes in branded D2C (not generic warehouse)
- [ ] Inbound inspection protocol: 2% sample check per shipment
- [ ] QC checklist completed on received inventory:
  - Linked toe seam present and smooth
  - Woven label position and alignment
  - Pair matching (weight, color consistency)
  - Dimensional check vs. spec sheet
- [ ] Shopify inventory connected to 3PL
- [ ] Subscription fulfillment tested: 5 internal test orders
- [ ] Editorial card final print run received and approved

### Capital Required

### 3PL Selection Criteria
- Experience with subscription box brands
- Pick/pack per unit: under $3.50
- Returns processing capability
- Location: EU-friendly (if targeting European market)

## Phase 4: Waitlist Launch → First Cohort
**Timeline:** Month 11 → Month 14

### Objective
Convert waitlist to Founding Members. Execute first subscription term with zero delivery failures.

### Founding Members Protocol
- First 100 subscribers = "Founding Members"
- Lifetime 10% discount, locked to original semester price
- Named in first editorial letter: "You are among the 100."
- Allocation: 2 pairs (choice of colorway) + physical editorial letter + Founding Member card

### Subscription Structure
| Tier | Cadence | Contents | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard Enrollment | Quarterly | 2 pairs + editorial letter | $65/term |
| Extended Enrollment | Semi-annual | 4 pairs + extended editorial + care kit | $120/term |

**Language rules:** "Enroll" not "subscribe." "Term" not "month." "Defer" not "cancel."

- Week 2: Open enrollment to general waitlist
- Week 3: Paid acquisition begins (Meta Advantage+, $20/day ceiling)

### Content Gate Before Paid Acquisition
Minimum live before spending $1 on ads:
- 3 Material Guides published
- 6 articles published

## Gold Discipline in Operations

The visual standards of the brand extend into every physical touchpoint.

| Touchpoint | Standard |
|---|---|
| Mailer box | Matte black, soft-touch. Embossed logo. No gold foil. |
| Tissue paper | Charcoal or ivory. No logo printing. |
| Editorial card | Cream, uncoated, 300gsm. Black text. Academy mark × 1 only. |
| Sock tag | White card. Fiber spec, micron count, country. No decoration. |
| Editorial letter | A5. Uncoated paper. Black text. No color print. No images. |
| Packaging tape | Black matte or plain. Never branded tape. |
| Return slip | Minimal. One line: "Return or defer your next term." |

**Rule:** Gold appears once per physical touchpoint, or not at all. Repetition signals insecurity.
